Trust is product infrastructure

Abundance should expand possibility without manufacturing certainty.

The production vision separates discovery from verification, eligibility from selection, and estimated value from guaranteed support.

01

Source provenance

Every live listing should retain its official source, source type, retrieval date, and verification history.

02

Visible uncertainty

Terms such as free, funded, subsidized, reimbursement-based, and competitive should never be collapsed into one claim.

03

Human-owned decisions

AI may help normalize or explain information. It should not declare final eligibility, guarantee selection, or apply without consent.

04

Privacy by restraint

Matching should use the least sensitive data necessary, with optional eligibility attributes separated from public profile data.
